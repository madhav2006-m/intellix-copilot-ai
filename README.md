# IntelliX Launchpad

Create a premium, modern SaaS landing page for a fictional AI technology startup called “IntelliX”.

Brand:

Name: IntelliX

Brand tagline: “Intelligence Beyond Limits.”

Product tagline: “Automate. Simplify. Grow.”

Product: AI Operations Copilot

Target customers: Small urban service businesses with medium technology adoption.

IMPORTANT DESIGN DIRECTION:

Make the website visually match a premium AI startup commercial. Use the same overall visual identity as the IntelliX sales video:

- Dark navy/near-black background

- Deep blue to purple gradients

- Subtle glowing purple/blue AI effects

- Glassmorphism cards

- Soft neon borders and shadows

- Clean white typography

- Premium futuristic SaaS aesthetic

- Minimal but visually impressive

- Smooth animations and transitions

- Professional, trustworthy, business-focused

- Responsive on desktop, tablet and mobile

Do NOT make it look like a generic template.

Do NOT use random stock photography.

Use abstract AI visuals, workflow diagrams, dashboard mockups and glowing technology elements.

PAGE STRUCTURE:

1. NAVIGATION BAR

Logo:

“IntelliX”

Navigation:

Home

How It Works

Features

Benefits

About

Right-side CTA:

“Book a Demo”

Make the navbar transparent/dark with a subtle blur effect.

Large headline:

“Your AI Operations Copilot”

“Automate everyday operations, reduce workload, and make smarter business decisions with IntelliX.”

Primary button:

“Book a Demo”

“Explore IntelliX”

The dashboard should visually show:

- Automated tasks

- Customer follow-ups

- Scheduling

- Documents

- Reports

- Productivity insights

Add subtle animated glowing connections around the dashboard.

Display:

“Intelligence Beyond Limits.”

3. PROBLEM SECTION

Heading:

“Running a business shouldn’t mean doing everything manually.”

Show four elegant glass cards:

“Repetitive Tasks”

“Customer Follow-ups”

“Scheduling”

“Reports & Documents”

Explain briefly that disconnected tools and manual workflows consume valuable time.

4. INTELLIX SOLUTION SECTION

Heading:

“Meet IntelliX”

Text:

“An affordable AI Operations Copilot designed to simplify everyday business operations.”

Show a large central AI dashboard.

Create animated workflow:

Task → IntelliX AI → Automated Action → Business Insight

5. FEATURES SECTION

Create six premium feature cards:

Automated Workflows

Customer Follow-ups

Smart Scheduling

Document Management

Business Reporting

AI-Powered Insights

Each card should have a simple modern line icon and subtle hover animation.

6. BENEFITS SECTION

Heading:

“Work Smarter. Grow Faster.”

Show four large metrics/benefits:

“Save Time”

“Reduce Workload”

“Improve Productivity”

“Make Smarter Decisions”

Use glowing number-style visual elements without inventing specific statistics.

7. HOW IT WORKS

Create a clean 3-step process:

01 — Connect

Connect your everyday business workflows.

02 — Automate

Let IntelliX handle repetitive operational tasks.

03 — Grow

Focus your time on customers, decisions and business growth.

Use glowing connecting lines between the three steps.

Create a large interactive-looking dashboard section titled:

“Everything your business needs. In one intelligent workspace.”

Show panels for:

Customers

Calendar

Documents

Reports

AI Insights

Use realistic UI elements but keep the dashboard clearly a concept/mockup.

9. CALL TO ACTION

Large centered section with glowing gradient background:

“Ready to simplify your business?”

Text:

“Let IntelliX automate the work behind your growth.”

Button:

“Book a Demo”

10. FOOTER

“Intelligence Beyond Limits.”

“Automate. Simplify. Grow.”

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:

- Fully responsive

- Smooth scrolling

- Modern hover animations

- Subtle scroll-reveal animations

- Glassmorphism

- Gradient glow effects

- Accessible contrast

- Fast-loading design

- Professional typography

- Buttons must work

- Navigation links must scroll to sections

- No broken images

- No external brand logos

- Use IntelliX branding consistently

COLOR DIRECTION:

Primary background: very dark navy

Secondary: deep blue

Accent: electric purple/blue

Text: white and light gray

Use gradients and glow effects sparingly for a premium appearance.

OVERALL FEEL:

The final website should look like the official website of a futuristic AI SaaS company that could realistically be shown alongside the IntelliX 58-second sales pitch video.

The website and video must feel like the SAME BRAND.
